Longevity & Support

We believe that there is a natual point in a games life, where it makes sense to hand the technical reins over to the community. We are committed to supporting our titles, and always designing them with the long term in mind. If there is ever a server component to a game of ours, be asured that it will be open sourced and available as soon as possible. We believe that community run and moderated servers will always beat out any efforts we make.
